**Title:** Tacit · the AI Apprentice

**Description (under 100 words):**

Tacit sits beside an expert while they work, asks why at the pauses, and turns what it learns into a tutor. The expert's screen shows when a judgment happened. A question opens only at a pause, and only about what is on screen. The Work Map locks when every open slot is filled with the expert's own words and the expert says yes. A new hire then works an unseen case. The tutor steps in before a wrong decision is saved, explains with that reasoning, and replays the expert's screen moment. The same map can load as rules an agent follows, and the agent stops where the expert would.

**One-liners:**

1. Timing is code, not a prompt.
2. It never asks what the screen already shows.
3. Only the expert's words fill a slot, and only an explicit yes locks the map.
4. Rescued and learned are different labels.
5. It sees a still at a time and keeps only what explains a decision.
