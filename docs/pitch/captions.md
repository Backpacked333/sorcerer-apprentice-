# Captions and shot lists

Burn every spoken line. Each video is at most 60 seconds, 1080p, H.264, system audio and mic. Workspace mode, browser zoom 110–125%. Do not speed the pauses. Cut between beats.

## Team (docs/05 §7.3)

Four faces. One sentence each: who you are, what you built. One shared line on the retiring-expert gap. End on the moonshot frame. Record before midnight. It does not depend on the code.

File names for raw takes: `beat-<n>-take-<k>.mov`.
